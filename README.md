# Joshua Ebinisi | Personal Portfolio

A responsive, mobile-first portfolio website showcasing my work in quantitative analytics, software engineering, algorithmic trading, and community leadership.

## Overview

This project serves as my digital resume and project showcase. It features a strict black and white minimalist design with no gradient colors or complex animations. 

Key sections include:
* **Technical Portfolio:** Highlights my Summer 2026 Quantitative Analytics Internship at PNC Bank, SEO Tech Developer LITE program experience, and projects using Python, C++, and MATLAB.
* **Algorithmic Trading:** Details my backtesting scripts, order flow analysis, and trading statistics.
* **Creative Projects:** Previews the December 2026 relaunch of my clothing brand, Out of Bounds, alongside my UGC video content.
* **Leadership:** Covers my role as the 2026 to 2027 NSBE Finance Chair at the University of Houston, my volunteer work at Streetwise Georgia, and my past presidency at Earth Savers.

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3 (Fluid typography, strict black and white color palette)
* Typography: Pragmatica Cond, Pragmatica, and Roboto Mono

   cd joshua-ebinisi-portfolio

2. **Run the bat file after each change**
